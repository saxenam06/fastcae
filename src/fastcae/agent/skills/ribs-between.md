# Ribs between things
when: ribs between two or more named things - a boss and a wall, two bosses, a bore and the walls

Ribs between things end on those things at both ends. They stand on a floor when one lies right
under the open space between them - or hang from a ceiling right over it; when neither does, they
are webs with nothing under them.

1. **Name each thing** (the skill on naming). "The wall" across from a named thing: `relate`
   `across` from it lists what it looks at across the open space, the most first, with the gap.
   Name what the words mean - every face of that wall that a good share looks at.
2. **What is under the space between them**: `relate` `between` with all of them. Its `stand_on`
   names the floor right under most of that space, or the ceiling right over it: the ribs stand on
   that. None, and the ribs stand on nothing - webs between them. Metal filling the space means
   they are one piece: nothing to put between them, which is worth an attention line.
3. **Write the block** with `edit_study`: `stand_on` that floor, or an empty list for nothing under
   them; `end_on` every thing named. Leave the pattern free unless the words choose one: spokes
   about the round one and straight webs across both stay open.
4. Look at where the ribs go, and say what stopped them if few are made.
