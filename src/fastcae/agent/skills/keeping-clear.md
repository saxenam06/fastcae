# Keeping clear of things
when: the words say not to cross, touch, go over, interfere with or keep away from something

One rule keeps clear of anything: `keep_clear_of`, naming what in `refs`, with `clearance_mm`.

- **What**: any face or feature by id; a boss or bore named by one of its faces - `describe` the
  face, and name the feature it is `in`; the holes found (every hole through the floor is read off
  the part and suggested already - `confirm` that rule when the words ask for it, add one of their
  own when they give another distance); the ribs of another block, as `ribs:` and the block's id.
- **Ribs already in place**: "without interfering with the other ribs" names the ribs of every block
  there before this one, one `ribs:` entry each.
- **How far**: as the words say. "Don't cross", "don't touch", "without interfering": no clearance -
  zero. "Keep away" with no distance: the smallest radius the part allows, and an attention line
  saying so.
- **Whose ribs keep clear**: words about the ribs being added hold for their block alone - put the
  rule in that block's `rules`, in the same edit that adds it. Words about ribs already there go in
  their block's `rules`, by its id. Only words about every rib, wherever it is, go in the edit's
  own `rules`: those hold for every block, now and to come.

The placement works out each entity's outline where the ribs are laid - a disc for something round,
its own outline for anything else - and drops every rib that would cross it; another block's ribs
count only where they stand at the same height.
