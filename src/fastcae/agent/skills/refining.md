# Refining, turn by turn
when: the engineer adds to or changes what they asked before - another place, a new limit, "also", "instead", "not there"

The study holds everything said so far. Read it first, and change only what the new words change.

- **Words that add ribs** ("add ribs…", "now also ribs on…"): a new block - even where an earlier
  block already puts ribs, and even between the same things. Earlier blocks stay as they are.
  "Without interfering with the ribs already there": the new block keeps clear of the ribs of every
  earlier block (the skill on keeping clear).
- **Words that change ribs already there** ("make those T-shaped", "no taller than…", "don't
  cross…", "instead", "not there"), or that answer what the attention lines asked: change that
  block, or give it a rule in its `rules`, by its id. Unclear which block: the latest, unless the
  words name the place.
- **"Also"**: add to what is there - another option for a setting, another entity to keep clear
  of. **"Instead"** or **"not…"**: replace, or `drop` the rule by its id.
- A rule the part suggested that the engineer now states as theirs: `confirm` it.
- Never take out what was accepted unless the words say so. Each turn is one `edit_study` where it
  can be: blocks, their rules, and attention together.
