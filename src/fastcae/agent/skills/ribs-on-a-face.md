# Ribs on a face
when: ribs on a face or a floor, with no pattern or place asked for beyond it

1. `describe` the face: it must be flat. A face given that is not flat is a wall or a round thing -
   then the ribs stand on what it rises from (`relate` `stands_on`), and that is worth an
   attention line.
2. Other flat faces in the same plane the words name, or that the engineer selected, stand in it
   too: one block standing on all of them.
3. **Write the block** with `edit_study`: stand on the face; leave `end_on` out - what rises round
   it is read off the part - and leave the pattern free: parallel, grid, triangle and spokes all
   stay open, unless the words choose one.
4. Look at where the ribs go in the edit's answer, and say in an attention line what stopped them
   if few are made.
